AC2:Unreal 0.11.2 for Windows x64

Human tutorial on Icefire

Drag the Imbued Digging Tool from your backpack onto the Shelter Scout or her name to give it to her. The selected Scout's name now accepts the drop.

Double-click the Menhir Glyph in your backpack, then click the Menhir Portal. The client waits for that click even if you had an enemy selected. Escape or a right-click in the world cancels targeting. Inside the shard room, speak to the Advance Shelter Scout and double-click the Menhir Shard to leave.

The hand-in and glyph requests were tested against Icefire and stock game data. Completion with a fresh character on live Icefire is still unverified.

To update from 0.11.1, open the launcher and choose Check for updates. Players on 0.11.0 can run this installer directly.
